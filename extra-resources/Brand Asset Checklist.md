# Brand Asset Checklist  
A simple guide for collecting brand elements required for website and marketing projects.

---

## 🎨 Brand Identity
- Primary logo (PNG)
- Secondary / alternate logo
- SVG logo (if available)
- Transparent background versions
- Icon / favicon versions

---

## 🎯 Colour System
- Primary colours (HEX)
- Secondary colours
- Accent colours
- Background / neutral colours

---

## 🔤 Typography
- Primary font
- Secondary font (if applicable)
- Font weights and usage
- Line height / spacing rules

---

## 🖼 Imagery
- Photography style (e.g., lifestyle, corporate, product-focused)
- Illustration guidelines
- Icon style (line, filled, geometric, etc.)
- Image filters or presets (optional)

---

## 📄 Brand Documents
- Brand guide or CI manual
- Mission, vision, values
- Messaging guidelines
- Tone of voice framework

---

## 🧩 Marketing Assets
- Business card design
- Brochure/flyer templates
- Social media templates
- Email signature
- Presentation templates

---

## ✔ Final Notes
Having these assets ready ensures a consistent and professional visual identity across all digital platforms.
