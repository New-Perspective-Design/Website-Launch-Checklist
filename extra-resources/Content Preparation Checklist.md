# Content Preparation Checklist  
A guide for gathering, organising, and preparing content before a website build begins.

---

## 📄 Page Content
- Final text for all pages (Home, About, Services, Contact, etc.)
- Page structure (H1, H2, bullet points, CTAs)
- Proofread and approved by client
- Tone and voice guidelines followed

---

## 🖼 Images & Media
- High-quality images (correct resolution for web)
- Logo files in PNG + SVG (if available)
- Team photos, product photos, event photos
- Videos optimised (MP4 or WebM)
- Stock images licensed if required

---

## 🎨 Branding Elements
- Brand colours (HEX / RGB)
- Font selections (Google Fonts preferred)
- Logo usage rules
- Brand icons or illustrations

---

## 📧 Contact Information
- Business email(s)
- Phone numbers
- WhatsApp number (optional)
- Physical address
- Social media links

---

## 🧭 Navigation Structure
- Primary menu layout
- Footer layout
- Subpages and dropdowns
- Internal linking strategy

---

## 📦 Additional Assets
- Downloads (PDFs, catalogues, menus, brochures)
- Product spreadsheets (if applicable)
- Service pricing tables
- Testimonials (approved)

---

## ✔ Final Notes
This checklist helps streamline the website build process, reduces revisions, and ensures consistent quality in all on-site content.
